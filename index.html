Live · Open source

TokenTelemetry

Understand what your agents actually did. One local dashboard for tokens, estimated cost and step-by-step traces across 21 coding and autonomous agents.

  • One command, no SDK. It reads the logs your agents already write. Nothing to wire into your code.
  • Your data stays on your machine. Prompts, logs and costs are read locally. Anonymous, content-free usage stats can be switched off.
  • Every agent in one place. Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI, Copilot, Hermes and more.

Straight answers

Before you ask.

What can you build for us right now?

Agentic applications, agent cost and observability work, and automation pipelines. If your project is outside that, tell us anyway and we will say plainly whether we are the right team.

How do we know the agent will actually work?

We start with a narrow pilot on your real task and share the traces, the costs and the failures. You decide whether to continue based on what you can see.

Is TokenTelemetry really free?

Yes. It is MIT licensed and runs on your own machine. The source is public on GitHub.

Do you build robots?

Not yet. Robotics is where FasterFold is headed, and the agent and measurement work we do today is the foundation for it. We will not claim otherwise until there is hardware to show.

Why does a software studio run a YouTube channel?

Because it is the fastest way for you to judge how we think. The channel is also made by our own agentic pipeline, so it is a working demo of what we build.
